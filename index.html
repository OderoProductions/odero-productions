Process

How a shoot works

Three steps, agreed up front, so you always know what happens next.

  1. The brief

    A call to pin down the audience, the message and the moments that matter. You get a plan, a shot list and a timeline before anything is booked.

  2. The shoot

    A focused day on location at your venue, office or site. Keynotes, interviews and b-roll captured without getting in anyone’s way.

  3. The delivery

    Polished edits in platform-ready formats, with subtitles and edited photos, on the turnaround agreed in the brief.

Questions

FAQs

Where are you based, and do you travel?

Worthing, Sussex. We film for clients across the UK, and travel is agreed in the brief.

Can you film on-site?

Yes. We film on location at your premises, your venue or anywhere else that helps tell the story.

Do you help with the concept?

Yes, creative direction is included. During the brief we work out the angles, the structure and the questions with you, so it fits your audience and what you’re offering.

What if we don’t want to be on camera?

No problem. We can use voiceover, or build the film around your work, your space and your event without talking heads.

Which formats do you deliver?

All the main ones: 16:9 for YouTube, your website and presentations, 1:1 for feeds, and 9:16 for Reels, TikTok and Shorts.

How fast is the turnaround?

It depends on the size of the project. The timeline is agreed during the brief and confirmed before the shoot.

Can you work alongside our marketing team?

Yes. We’re happy to work with your in-house team or your agency so the films fit the wider plan.
