Services

What we film

One crew from first call to final delivery. Every shoot gets a plan, a shot list and one person to call.

  • Conferences & summits

    Keynotes, panels and the conversations in the corridor, cut into a highlights film and clips for social.

  • Launches & awards nights

    Low light, big moments, no second takes. Your running order becomes our shot list, so nothing gets missed.

  • Brand & company films

    Who you are and why it matters, in two minutes your team will actually want to share.

  • Interviews & testimonials

    Clients and leaders on camera, properly lit and edited to sound like themselves on a good day.

  • Short-form for social

    Vertical edits with captions and on-screen text, ready for Instagram, LinkedIn and paid ads.

  • Photography

    Edited stills from the same shoot, for the website, the press release and the post the next morning.

Process

How a shoot works

Three steps, agreed up front, so you always know what happens next.

Call sheet Odero Productions Your shoot
StepWhat happensWhat you get
1 The brief A call to pin down the audience, the message and the moments that matter most. A plan, a shot list and a timeline, before anything is booked.

Questions

Before you book

Where are you based, and do you travel?

Worthing, Sussex. We film for clients across the UK, and travel is agreed in the brief.

Can you film on-site?

Yes. We film at your premises, your venue or anywhere else that helps tell the story.

Do you help with the concept?

Yes, creative direction is included. During the brief we work out the angles, the structure and the questions with you, so the film fits your audience.

What if we don’t want to be on camera?

No problem. We can use voiceover, or build the film around your work, your space and your event, with no talking heads.

Which formats do you deliver?

All the main ones: 16:9 for YouTube, your website and presentations, 1:1 for feeds, and 9:16 for Reels, TikTok and Shorts.

How fast is the turnaround?

It depends on the size of the project. The timeline is agreed during the brief and confirmed before the shoot.

Can you work alongside our marketing team?

Yes. We’re happy to work with your in-house team or your agency so the films fit the wider plan.
