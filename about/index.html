Meet the founder

“It goes back further than you’d think.”

I’m Christopher, the founder of Odero Productions. I studied media and creative production, then took a degree in sports broadcasting, and had a short spell filming for a Premier League and WSL club before putting everything into my own studio at the start of 2024.

Today I film conferences, launches, brand films and interviews for businesses across Sussex and the UK, from a base in Worthing. I plan, shoot and edit, and bring in extra crew when a job needs more hands.

My story

How it started

The origin story of Odero Productions, in under two minutes. Or read it below.

  1. About ten years ago

    A PS4 and a laptop on its last legs

    I was recording gameplay and commentary videos on my PS4 and cutting them together on a laptop that was barely holding on. The gaming channels of the time were the inspiration. Mostly, it was just fun.

  2. School and university

    An education built around it

    I liked it enough to build my whole education around it: media studies, then creative production, then a degree in sports broadcasting. I’d always been into sport, and media had taken over, so it was the perfect match.

  3. After graduating

    The long way round

    I didn’t go straight into broadcasting. I worked in a warehouse, saved up and travelled around Spain for a while. Once I was back, I filmed local football matches when the chance came up.

  4. Late 2023

    The dream job (or so I thought)

    Filming and editing for a Premier League and WSL club, on the kind of kit I used to daydream about owning.

  5. Three months later

    Handing in my notice

    I realised it wasn’t for me long term. It was hard to build anything of my own alongside it, I couldn’t share the work, and the schedule left little room for anything else. I was putting my energy into something that wasn’t really mine. Looking back, I could have given it longer, but I walked away and backed myself instead.

  6. Start of 2024

    Odero Productions, full time

    Everything went into Odero Productions. Now I film events, brand films and interviews for businesses across Sussex and the UK. Still here. Still going.
