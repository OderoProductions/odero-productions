Privacy

Privacy notice

Last updated 18 November 2026

Odero Productions is a video production business based in Worthing, Sussex. This page explains what personal information is handled when you use this website or get in touch.

This website

  • The site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising trackers.
  • There are no forms on the site, so it collects nothing you type.
  • Fonts and images are served from the site itself.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub records technical details such as IP addresses to keep the service secure. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Videos

Films on the work pages are played through Cloudflare Stream. Nothing is loaded from Cloudflare until you press play. When you do, Cloudflare receives technical details such as your IP address in order to deliver the video. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy.

When you get in touch

  • Booking a call opens Calendly, which collects your name, email address and anything you add to the booking. See Calendly’s privacy notice.
  • Email and WhatsApp messages are kept so we can reply, quote for and deliver your project.

Your details are used only to respond to you and to carry out work you have asked for. They are never sold or passed on for marketing.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete the information held about you. Email info@oderoproductions.com and we will sort it out. If you are unhappy with the response, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.
